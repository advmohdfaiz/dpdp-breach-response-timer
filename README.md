# DPDP Breach Response Timer & Incident Workflow Engine

An interactive, open-access legal-tech compliance engine operationalizing statutory obligations under **Section 8(6)** of the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023)** and **Rule 7** of the DPDP Rules.

Designed for Data Fiduciaries, Data Protection Officers (DPOs), and privacy counsel managing incident response triage in India.

---

## Key Features

- **Dual Statutory Timelines:**
  - **Data Principals Intimation:** Tracks the mandate under Section 8(6) to intimate each affected Data Principal without undue delay.
  - **Data Protection Board (DPBI) Reporting:** Live countdown for the 72-hour regulatory reporting deadline under Section 8(6) and Rule 7.
- **Statutory Risk & Severity Scoring:** Factors data categories, volume, breach classification under Section 2(u), and penalty evaluation criteria under Section 33(2).
- **Statutory Penalty Reference:** Comprehensive breakdown of **The Schedule [See Section 33(1)]**, including exposure up to ₹250 Crore for failure to maintain reasonable security safeguards under Section 8(5), and up to ₹200 Crore for failure to give breach notices under Section 8(6).
- **Automated Notice Generator:** Auto-generates statutorily aligned draft intimations for affected individuals and formal regulatory reports for the Board with 1-click clipboard copy and `mailto:` integration.
- **Incident Response Checklist:** 5-step structured workflow mapping Sections 8(4), 8(5), 8(6), 8(7), and 27(1)(a) with live audit logging.
- **Zero Data Collection:** Runs entirely client-side in the browser via vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript with local storage persistence.

---

## Statutory Authorities (Gazette of India, 11 August 2023)

- **Section 2(u):** Definition of *"personal data breach"*.
- **Section 8(4) & 8(5):** Obligation to implement technical and organisational measures and reasonable security safeguards.
- **Section 8(6):** Obligation to intimate personal data breaches to the Board and affected Data Principals.
- **Section 27(1)(a):** Inquiries and urgent mitigation directions by the Data Protection Board.
- **Section 33(1) & (2):** Quantum determination and mitigating factors (Section 33(2)(e)).
- **The Schedule:** Statutory penalty schedule (Items 1 to 7).
